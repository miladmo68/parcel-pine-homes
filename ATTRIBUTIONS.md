## Product research

Original implementation informed by current public product patterns:

- REALTOR.ca map/list search, property-type filters, favourites, and saved searches
- Zillow affordability filters, comparison signals, collections, saved search, and tour-planning patterns
- Open-source MERN real-estate repositories were reviewed only for capability benchmarking; no source code was copied

All product copy, brand, interface, data structures, code, and interaction design in Parcel & Pine are original.
